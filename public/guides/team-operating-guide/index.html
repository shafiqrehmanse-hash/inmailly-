1. Getting started

Your team workspace is at www.inmailly.com/team. Bookmark it on your phone and laptop.

New members — register

  1. Go to /team/register (you need an invite code from admin).
  2. Fill in full name, email, WhatsApp/phone (with country code, e.g. +92…), password, and invite code.
  3. Click Create account →
  4. Check your email and click Verify email & join team.
  5. Return to /team/login and sign in.

2. Recommended daily workflow

Scripts→ Work Links→ Open LinkedIn profile→ Send outreach→ Mark complete→ Add lead→ Log replies
Golden rule: Never skip marking a link complete after you use it — that keeps your stats accurate and frees the pipeline for the team.

4. Scripts (/team/scripts)

Copy outreach text before you open Work Links. Tap a script to expand it.

  1. Open Scripts in the sidebar.
  2. Tap Add Note (LinkedIn connection note, max 350 chars) or InMail (Sales Navigator, up to 1,900 chars).
  3. Use Copy on Subject and Message separately.
  4. Paste into LinkedIn when you run outreach on a Usual link (manual mode).

5. Work Links (/team/links)

This is where you get LinkedIn profile URLs to work on.

Get links from the pool

ButtonWhat it doesRules
Get Usual linksStandard manual outreach with ScriptsUp to 35 links; need fewer than 5 active Usual links first
Get Intelligence linksAI writes custom InMail from screenshotUp to 20 links; must have 0 active Intelligence links before requesting more

Three tabs

  • Available — pool links you can claim
  • My active — links assigned to you now
  • Used — completed links (history)

Claim a link

  1. On Available, click Claim link.
  2. Choose mode:
    • ✦ Intelligence (recommended) — AI personalized InMail from profile screenshot
    • Usual — open profile and paste Scripts manually
  3. Click Open profile ↗ to open LinkedIn in a new tab.

Usual link — complete

  1. Open profile → send Add Note or InMail using Scripts.
  2. Back on InMailly, click Mark complete.
  3. Optionally click + Add as lead if they responded or you want to track them.

6. Intelligence InMail (screenshot → AI)

For Intelligence links only. The AI reads the profile from your screenshot and writes a custom subject + InMail body.

  1. Click Open LinkedIn profile ↗ on the link card.
  2. On the profile, take a screenshot:
    • Windows: Win + Shift + S → crop the profile area
    • Mac: Cmd + Shift + 4
  3. On InMailly, click ✦ Paste screenshot (or Ctrl+V in the modal).
  4. Click ✦ Generate personalized InMail
  5. Copy Subject and InMail body → paste into LinkedIn InMail.
  6. After sending on LinkedIn, click Sent — mark complete.
Tip: Crop the screenshot to show name, headline, and about section — not the whole screen. Better crop = better message.
Important: Always click Sent — mark complete after you send on LinkedIn. Your used-link count and leaderboard depend on it.

7. My Leads (/team/leads)

Log everyone who responds or is worth tracking.

Add a new lead

  1. Open My Leads in the sidebar (or + Add as lead from a Work Link).
  2. Fill in: First name (required), Last name, LinkedIn URL, Email, Phone.
  3. Set Response status (New, Contacted, Replied, Interested, etc.).
  4. Add notes: what they said, next step, meeting time.
  5. Click Add Lead →

From Work Links (prefilled)

When you click + Add as lead on a link card, the LinkedIn URL is filled automatically. You only add name + status + notes.

Lead statuses

Lead thread (conversation log)

  1. Click 💬 Thread on any lead row.
  2. Add messages — toggle sender: team (you) or lead (prospect).
  3. When the lead sends a message, set sender to lead → status auto-updates to Replied.
  4. When deal wins, check Deal closed — team celebration banner may appear for 24h.

8. Responses & Reply Assistant

Responses (/team/responses)

Shows leads who replied, are interested, or need follow up. Open any card → View thread to continue the conversation.

Reply Assistant (/team/reply-assistant)

AI helps you reply to LinkedIn threads from a screenshot.

  1. Click + New to create a thread (prospect name + optional company/LinkedIn URL).
  2. Screenshot the LinkedIn conversation (Win+Shift+S).
  3. Paste screenshot in Reply Assistant (Ctrl+V).
  4. Click ✦ Generate reply or 📅 Meeting link reply if sending a booking link.
  5. Edit the draft if needed → Copy reply → paste on LinkedIn.
  6. Click ✓ Mark sent (or Mark sent + meeting booked).
Reply Assistant flow
1 Paste screenshot
2 Generate reply
3 Copy → send on LinkedIn
4 Mark sent (keeps your thread history)

9. Team performance (/team/performance)

Leaderboard for the whole team. Your rank is based on links used, leads, deals closed, and referrals.

  • Intelligence links score slightly higher than Usual links.
  • Deals closed give the biggest score boost.
  • Check Your rank at the top and sort the table by Productivity, Used, Leads, or Deals.
Focus on consistent daily output: claim links → complete them → log leads → follow up on responses.

10. Earn & Refer (/team/referrals)

  1. Open Earn & Refer in the sidebar.
  2. Copy your shareable link or referral code.
  3. Share with people who might join as outreach workers.
  4. When they register and convert, you earn (shown in Earnings history).

11. Settings (/team/settings)

  • Upload profile photo — circular avatar on leaderboard (prompt appears until you upload).
  • WhatsApp / phone — keep updated so admin and team leader can reach you.
  • Name and email changes require admin — contact your team leader.

12. Offer letter & Info Doc (when admin requests)

Red/amber banners on Home mean action is required.

Offer letter (/team/contract)

  1. Read the agreement scroll box.
  2. Check the confirmation box.
  3. Sign with your finger/mouse on the signature pad.
  4. Click Sign & submit agreement.

Info Doc (/team/info-doc)

Upload ID photos, address, emergency contact, education, and employment details when admin sends the request. Click Submit Info Doc when complete.
